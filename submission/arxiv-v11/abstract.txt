The freely generated zero-identity Boolean category of Došen and Petrić is maximal: every additional equation between distinct parallel arrows, closed under the arrow operations and uniform formula substitution, makes the quotient a preorder. This disproves the nonmaximality conjecture in Section~14.2 of the September 2007 revision of Proof-Theoretical Coherence. Boolean Coherence reduces a new equation to a contextual equation involving one literal. Uniform substitution extends that equation to every formula. Two isomorphisms of Došen and Petrić then force the product projections to coincide. A Lean formalization proves coherence for the equation-generated presentation and verifies maximality, the model corollary, and occurrence substitution in finite-ordinal coordinates.
