Abstract:
We prove that the zero-identity Boolean category of Do\v{s}en and
Petri\'c, freely generated by a nonempty set of propositional variables,
is maximal. Every congruence preserved by the operations on morphisms
and by uniform formula substitution either is equality or identifies
every pair of parallel morphisms. This disproves their conjecture that
the category is not maximal. We give two proofs that use Boolean
Coherence and uniform formula substitution to derive equality of the
product projections from an identification of distinct parallel
morphisms. We also give a Lean formalization of these proofs and their
dependencies. It includes Boolean Coherence and the correspondence
between substitution on tagged occurrence sets and substitution on
finite ordinals.
