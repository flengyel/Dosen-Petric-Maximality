We prove that the zero-identity Boolean category of Došen and Petrić freely generated by a nonempty set of propositional variables is maximal. Adjoining an equation between distinct parallel arrows and closing under the arrow operations and uniform formula substitution identifies all parallel arrows. This disproves the conjecture in Section 14.2 of the September 2007 revision of Proof-Theoretical Coherence. Boolean Coherence and selection of occurrences reduce the added equation to a contextual equation on a signed literal. Substitution gives this equation for every formula. Two isomorphisms listed by Došen and Petrić then yield equality of the product projections. The Lean formalization proves coherence, maximality and the model corollary for the generated presentation, as well as the substitution equations in tagged and finite-ordinal coordinates.
