2. EXACT MATHEMATICAL TARGET

Let P be a nonempty set of propositional variables. B(P) is the free
zero-identity Boolean category of Došen–Petrić, Proof-Theoretical Coherence,
September 2007 revision, §§14.2–14.3. A proof congruence is an equivalence
relation on each hom-set compatible with composition and every arrow
operation of that presentation. A substitution congruence also respects
uniform substitution of formulas throughout arrow terms and their types.

The target theorem says: if a substitution congruence identifies distinct
parallel arrows, it identifies every parallel pair. Equivalently, its quotient is
thin. This establishes maximality and contradicts the book's conjectured
nonmaximality, provided the formulation matches the book's schematic
equational extensions. Verify that correspondence explicitly.

Do not substitute a theorem about derivability, an arbitrary hom-set
relation, or the kernel of one fixed valuation. The semantic corollary
quantifies over every interpretation of propositional variables.

Use the actual zero-identity law:

  f ∘ 0_A = 0_C ∘ g                  for parallel f,g : A → C.

Here 0_A : A → A is the specified zero endomorphism. A zero arrow 0_{A,C}
is defined as f ∘ 0_A only when an arrow f : A → C already exists.
Prove independence of that witness and absorption. Do not install a
blanket HasZeroMorphisms instance: it supplies arrows in every hom-set
and changes this presentation.

The distributor has type

  d_{A,B,C} : A ∧ (B ∨ C) → (A ∧ B) ∨ C

and satisfies

  d_{A,B,C} ∘ (1_A ∧ ι₁^{B,C}) = ι₁^{A∧B,C}.

The faithful occurrence functor G maps formulas to finite sets of tagged
positions and arrows to binary relations. Repeated occurrences of a
variable are separate positions. Products and coproducts both have
disjoint-union occurrence sets. Constants have empty occurrence sets.
Specify the generator relations, including the distributor's rebracketing
relation; prove the calculations used below.

3. PROOF TO FORMALIZE

Start with §5. For j_A = ι₁^{A,⊤}, define the contextual equation K_A:

  j_A ∘ π₁^{A,⊥} ≡ 0_{A∧⊥,A∨⊤}.

Define

  u_A^B = d_{A,B,A} ∘ (1_A ∧ ι₂^{B,A}),
  h_A^B = [π₁^{A,B},1_A] ∘ u_A^B : A ∧ A → A.

The occurrence relation of h_A^B folds both tagged copies onto Occ(A).
Consequently coherence makes it independent of B and invariant under
the swap τ_A = ⟨π₂,π₁⟩. Write it h_A.

Assuming K_A for every A, establish the following chain:

  a. Evaluate h_A at B = ⊥. Using K_A and the explicitly proved relation
     calculation, obtain j_A ∘ h_A ≡ j_A ∘ π₂.
  b. Symmetry gives j_A ∘ π₁ ≡ j_A ∘ π₂.
  c. Precompose with ⟨1_A,0_A⟩ to get j_A ≡ ι₂^{A,⊤} ∘ !_A.
  d. Prove that A ∨ ⊤ is isomorphic to ⊤ modulo the congruence, using
     the coproduct equations. By symmetry, ⊤ ∨ A is terminal too.
  e. Evaluate the same h_A at B = ⊤ and apply the first-injection
     equation to obtain h_A ≡ π₁.
  f. Its symmetry gives π₁ ≡ π₂. Pairing with any parallel f,g then
     gives f ≡ g.

Work directly with congruence relations initially if this simplifies the
proof. Construct the quotient and its inherited products/coproducts when
needed. Do not cancel j_A or apply faithfulness of G to the quotient:
coherence is used in the original category, and its equations descend.

Next formalize §§3–4. Normalize to signed literals, select an edge in
Gf differing from Gg, and construct the selector and coselector isolating
it. This produces K_ℓ for a signed literal ℓ. Positive literals transfer
by p ↦ A; negative literals transfer by p ↦ ¬A followed by the specified
double-negation isomorphism. Prove the occurrence/substitution transport,
including the empty and repeated-occurrence cases.

Keep three milestones distinct:

  I. Contextual collapse, including the finite-relation calculations,
     relative to an explicit structural and faithful-interpretation
     interface.
  II. Maximality relative to explicitly stated coherence, normalization,
      and presentation/substitution inputs.
  III. Maximality for the actual Došen–Petrić presentation, with those
       inputs proved or supplied by an existing proved formalization
       and a checked bridge to its definitions.

Milestones I and II are useful formal results, but their theorem names,
README, Challenge statements, and submission metadata must identify their
hypotheses. Do not call them an unconditional formalization of III.
Formalizing all of Boolean Coherence may be the largest part of III.
