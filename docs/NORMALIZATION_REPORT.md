## Construction and proof separation

`form A false` normalizes A; `form A true` normalizes ¬A. Recursion is on A
alone. A negation flips the Boolean sign. A negative conjunction becomes a
disjunction of negative recursive forms; a negative disjunction becomes a
conjunction. Positive constants remain themselves; their negative forms are
the opposite constants. Variables produce the corresponding signed literals.
Thus arbitrary nesting terminates without a contravariant arrow-negation
functor or an unproved naturality assertion.

`NormalComparison A B` packages explicitly constructed arrows and their
inverse laws, inverse occurrence coordinates, graph laws, and signed-label
correspondence. Every field is supplied by the recursive constructors.
`inverse_comp` proves cancellation of composed inverse pairs using only
category laws. `conj/disj` use tensor composition and tensor identity laws.
The five base comparisons use the existing ten source inverse equations.
Consequently the arrow inverse proofs never infer arrow equality from relation
equality. Graph and label proofs are separate fields, proved using the existing
occurrence assignment and elementary coordinate calculations. No exhaustive
defining-equation proof was expanded or rebuilt from scratch for this task.

`Formula.signedLabel A x` is the variable with the parity of all negations
on the path to the tagged position x; `Formula.polarity` extracts that parity.
The signed label's letter agrees with the existing `Formula.label`, and on
normal forms it agrees with `NormalForm.literalAt`.
