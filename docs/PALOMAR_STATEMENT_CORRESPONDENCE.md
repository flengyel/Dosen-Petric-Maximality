# The selected maximality statements

Fix a universe `u` and a type `P : Type u` of propositional variables.
`Formula P` is generated by variables, top, bottom, conjunction, disjunction
and negation. `Presentation.Term P A B` is the primitive typed arrow grammar.
Its composition `Term.comp f g` applies `f` and then `g`. `Equation` is the
complete generating theory, and `Derives` is its equivalence closure under
composition and both tensors. Thus raw syntax equality and generated arrow
equality are distinct notions.

A `Palomar.PrimitiveCongruence P` is an equivalence relation on each type of
parallel raw terms. It contains every `Equation` and is preserved by composition
and both tensors. For `σ : P → Formula P`, `Term.subst σ` substitutes in the
whole arrow and both endpoint formulas. `R.Closed` requires preservation of the
relation by every such substitution. `R.Total` means that `R.Rel k l` holds for
every existing pair `k l : Term P A B`, for every `A` and `B`.

## Descent and restriction

The actual hom-type is the quotient of `Term P A B` by `Derives`. Write `ofTerm`
for its quotient map. `R.of_derives` proves that generated equality is contained
in `R.Rel`. The relation

`R.quotientRel (ofTerm f) (ofTerm g) ↔ R.Rel f g`

is well defined: changing either representative by `Derives` changes neither
side, using symmetry and transitivity of `R.Rel`. The resulting `R.fragment`
respects the actual composition, pairing and copairing operations. Pairing is
represented by a diagonal followed by the conjunction tensor; copairing uses
the disjunction tensor followed by a codiagonal. `fragment_closed` proves
uniform substitution closure on the quotient.

Conversely, `ofFull Q` restricts a full `Presentation.ProofCongruence P` to
raw terms. `ofFull_closed` preserves substitution closure. A closed primitive
congruence extends to a full proof congruence by `R.full`; its underlying relation
is unchanged. The previously proved fragment dichotomy supplies compatibility
with the derived operations, including negation. The declarations
`ofFull_full_rel` and `full_ofFull_rel` prove both inverse pointwise relation
correspondences. These are correspondences of congruence relations; no equality
of chosen structure records is asserted.

`full_total_iff` and `ofFull_total_iff` preserve totality. `raw_distinct_iff`
identifies `¬ Derives f g` with `ofTerm f ≠ ofTerm g`. Hence the raw and quotient
formulations use precisely the same additional equations between distinct
arrows. The extension through the fragment dichotomy is needed for the
full-congruence correspondence; the two selected maximality proofs below act
on `R.fragment` directly.

## Five selected declarations

All names in this table have prefix `DosenPetric.Palomar`.

| Declaration | Hypotheses and conclusion | Actual proof route |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `maximality` | `[Nonempty P]`, `R.Closed`, parallel `f,g`, `¬ Derives f g`, and `R.Rel f g` imply `R.Total`. | Quotient descent and `Presentation.fragment_maximality`, with the actual source interpretation, normalization, signed labels, substitution and double negation. This is the symmetric-fold route. |
| `short_maximality` | The same hypotheses and conclusion. | `Isolation.normalized_literal_context`, `ContextualCollapse.all_contexts`, and `ShortProof.total`, using the particular q/e arrows. |
| `congruence_dichotomy` | `[Nonempty P]` and `R.Closed` imply pointwise equivalence of `R.Rel` with `Derives`, or `R.Total`. | If some related pair is not derivably equal, apply `maximality`; otherwise use `of_derives` for the reverse inclusion. |
| `identity_ne_zero` | For each supplied `p : P`, `¬ Derives (Term.id (.var p)) (Term.zero (.var p))`. | The actual quotient witness `Presentation.identity_ne_primitive_zero`. |
| `no_top_to_bot` | For every `P : Type u`, `¬ Nonempty (Term P .top .bot)`. | Map any putative raw term into the actual quotient and apply `Presentation.empty_hom`. |

The first three statements retain the manuscript's nonempty-variable hypothesis.
The underlying actual-source maximality theorems are stronger and do not need
it. The identity witness takes its variable explicitly, and the empty-hom
statement has no nonemptiness hypothesis.

The selected short proof uses neither the selected fold theorem nor the
closed-fragment extension to manufacture totality. Its contextual equations
come from the actual source isolation argument, and `ShortProof.total` supplies
the q/e collapse. Empty hom-sets remain empty: totality relates existing parallel
arrows and does not assert their existence. In particular, `no_top_to_bot` is
compatible with maximality and with the nonzero identity witness.

The selected family corresponds to manuscript Theorem 2.1 and its two structural
witnesses. The complete-paper claim map additionally covers coherence, the model
corollary, substitution constructions and all appendices. Those additional
results remain proved in the substantive library; the five-name configuration
selects the family above.
