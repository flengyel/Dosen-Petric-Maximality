EXACT LEFT TARGET

For arbitrary P, formula C, contexts Γ, Δ, Θ, Λ, U, V, and existing proofs

  f : G P Γ (C :: Δ)
  g : G P (C :: Θ) Λ
  w : G P U V,

let a : ProofAddress g w and x : At U C.
Assume ReducibleL f g a x.

Produce h : G P U V such that:

  h.CutFree
  ofTerm h.denote = ofTerm w.denote

and, writing

  g' = a.replace h
  a' = a.inserted h,

prove for every context pair I,J, proof v : G P I J,
address b : ProofAddress h v, and position y : At I C that

  ReducibleL f g' (a'.append b) y
    implies
  v.rho .left y < w.rho .left x.

Prove the separately typed right analogue: replace an addressed right
contraction in f, use ReducibleR relative to the rebuilt cut, and compare
right-side ranks.

This is the proposed source-shaped target. Verify its correspondence to the
source and the existing definitions before asserting that it formalizes the
source lemma. The conclusion quantifies over EVERY reducible contraction
inside h after replacement, including surviving old descendants, not only
newly introduced contraction nodes.
