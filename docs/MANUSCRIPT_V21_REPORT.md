## Revised abstract

We prove that the zero-identity Boolean category of Došen and Petrić, freely generated by a nonempty set of propositional variables, is maximal. Adjoining any equation between parallel arrows that does not follow from the defining equations, and closing under the arrow operations and uniform formula substitution, collapses this category to a preorder. This disproves their conjecture that the category is not maximal. The proof uses Boolean Coherence to select a pair of variable occurrences on which the two arrows differ. Composition with arrows selecting these occurrences, uniform substitution and two isomorphisms yield equality of the product projections. We also prove that any zero-identity Boolean category satisfying such an additional equation under every interpretation of the variables is a preorder. The Lean formalization proves coherence, maximality and this model corollary for the category presented by the defining equations. It also establishes the correspondence between substitution on tagged occurrence sets and substitution on finite ordinals.
