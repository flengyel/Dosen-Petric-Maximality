`zeroConstruction_structural` also proves exact agreement with the manuscript’s
pairing/copairing version of the unit arrows. The constructed zero follows the typed path
A → A ∧ top → A ∧ (neg A ∨ A) → (A ∧ neg A) ∨ A → bottom ∨ A → A.
The arrows are rightIn, id ∧ η, d, ε ∨ id, and leftOut. First `(0I)` and
initiality give zero_bottom = id and uniqueness of existing arrows into bottom.
Thus ε absorbs precomposition by zero_A ∧ id. Unit, tensor, and distributor
naturality give a square along the displayed path, showing that its composite
is unchanged by precomposition with zero_A. Applying `(0I)` to the composite
and id_A identifies it with primitive zero_A. This proof is internal to the
source-generated quotient and does not use occurrence faithfulness.
