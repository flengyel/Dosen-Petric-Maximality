## (C) Minor issues

The Lean composite `comp f g` means first f, then g. The map and report state
this before displaying inverse laws; otherwise the two identity endpoints
could be misread. The source displays on pp.310/312 are unnumbered inverse
equations, not new equation labels invented for this implementation.

The name “normalization” denotes negation normal form: it does not eliminate
constants or identify all isomorphic formulas. `Formula.polarity` is the parity
of negations along an occurrence path; it is not the outer connective's sign.
Both conventions are now stated with the relevant declarations.

## (D) Cross-section consistency failures

No remaining drift was found between the new implementation, normalization
report, source table, and current statement/coverage/continuation documents.
The previous reports remain historical records, and the validation document
marks their earlier open-normalization status as historical.

The ambient category is `Presentation.category P`: objects are `Formula P`,
arrows are equivalence classes generated by the published equations, and
composition is covariant. Equality is never defined by occurrence relations.
`normal : Formula P → NormalForm P` is an object assignment; no contravariant
arrow operation or source-normalization functor is silently supplied.
`transport f` has type `Hom (normal A).formula (normal B).formula` for an
existing `f : Hom A B`. Its injectivity follows from inverse arrows in the
original category, not from any faithfulness premise or quotient cancellation.

The critical cases are visible in the recursion: positive/negative variables,
both constants, a negation below a negation, and both De Morgan cases.
Coordinate inverse laws hold at Empty as well as nonempty occurrence types.
`repeated_negative_tags p` proves the two tags below `¬(p ∧ p)` remain distinct.
These are proofs, not random tests. `P` need not be inhabited or decidable.
No zero arrow is fabricated in an empty hom-set. A comparison between
isomorphic objects cannot be generalized to an arbitrary backward arrow in a
two-object category with just one nonidentity arrow; no such claim is made.

The exact source digest and printed-to-PDF offset agree with the previous
presentation map. Primitive n/r/rho arrows are used as already constructed;
the new comparisons are derived compositions and tensors. Naturality is not
an available generator. The p.314 Auxiliary Lemma and categorical equivalence
are not asserted as consequences of this checkpoint.
