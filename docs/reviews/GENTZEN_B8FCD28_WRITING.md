was consulted for §1, points 5 and 10–17, printed pp.2–3, and §4, printed p.9.
These passages concern qualifications, coherent sentences and terminology,
visible mathematical structure, and the placement of definitions. The
project's explicit-typing, TikZ and process-prose requirements are additional
requirements; they are not attributed verbatim to KLR.
