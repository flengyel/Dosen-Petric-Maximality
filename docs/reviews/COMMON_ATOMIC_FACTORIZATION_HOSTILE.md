## (C) Minor issues

The terms called positive refer to arrow constructors, not positive literal
signs. That convention is stated before the grammar. The report distinguishes
the initial positive middle, the two padded but unmasked middles, and the final
masked middles. This distinction is necessary: factor equality alone would not
exclude auxiliary edges.

The source's mathematical composition order and Lean's execution order are
stated before the factor tables. Initial/terminal uniqueness is reused only
inside Reduced. Every zero is an endomorphism; composites with other endpoints
have explicitly typed existing arrows.

## (D) Cross-section consistency failures

No remaining mismatch was found between code, common-factor report and current
scope maps. Historical reports/access records, manuscript v4, old source files,
toolchain and manifest are unchanged. The 21 raw schemas and 61 reduced equation
schemas remain byte-identical. The common factorization accepts arbitrary P
and any two parallel raw terms, with no relation-equality premise.

The identity-weight port operation has object action expand and arrow action
map, repeatedly tensoring an existing arrow with port identities. The argument
proves and uses its naturality squares with the outer factors. No separate
functor record, monoidal equivalence, inverse operation or contravariant
negation is claimed. Zero-weight padding is used only through its separately
proved absorption and relation laws; it is not called an identity-preserving

In the two-object one-arrow test, none of these constructions selects an arrow
in an arbitrary empty hom-set. They start with existing raw terms and typed
structural/zero maps. Empty original occurrence types give no witnesses in the
support existential, so all corresponding middle edges vanish. This is a
relation calculation, not an equality-from-empty-relations argument.

2. Exact repeated-occurrence lists and terminating removal: met by atomicView,
   list equations, remainder_lt and atomicFactor.
3. Shared endpoints, prefix and suffix for arbitrary parallel raw terms:
   met by commonAtomicFactor and its two unconditional quotient equations.
4. Proven structural compatibility for padding/reassociation actually used:
   met by padding naturality, source separation, remap, category/tensor laws
   and product projections. No unproved list permutation is required.
5. Injective common embeddings and full auxiliary-edge exclusion:
   met by embed_injective, masked_support and both support fields.
6. Full middle-relation equality from original relation equality:
   met by relations_equal, not merely an embedded restriction.
7. The positive coherence premise must remain explicit and unsupplied:
   met by PositiveCoherence and the conditional transfer theorem.
8. Verification/source fidelity must have retained evidence:
   met by 46 files, 2816 audited declarations, all five rejected controls,
   pinned source hashes and manuscript integrity evidence.

Failure of 1–6 or 8 would require withdrawing the corresponding unconditional
claim. Failure of 7 would require withdrawing a promoted coherence/maximality
claim. No broader claim is licensed by this audit.
