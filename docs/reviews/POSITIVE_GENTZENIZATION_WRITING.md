## Definition order and notation audit

NormalForm, Term, Positive and the existing Reduced quotient precede this
work. Context folds and Iso precede append/exchange; At and Route precede
selected contraction. sourceConj and the actual mixed composites precede G
and denotation. Atomic versus compound identity/zero witnesses are explicitly
distinguished before the formula recursion. Represented is defined before
its closure lemmas and the exhaustive positive induction.

SingletonOcc/From introduce actual coordinate types before the occurrence
statement. CutCount/CutFree are distinguished from Topmost and Atomized;
none is called a rank or a decreasing measure for elimination. The source
rightmost versus first-copy operations are named with their type convention
and explicit transports. “Positive” refers to arrow constructors, with both
signed literal values allowed. These are confirmed definitions, not findings
of missing notation.
