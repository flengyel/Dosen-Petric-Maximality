- “Atomized constrains only thinning formulas.” This preserves compound cuts,
  contractions and endpoints.

  to prove both complete omitted schemas.” This separates the typed result

  monotonicity, nor a proof-size bound, is claimed.” This preserves the exact

  consequence.” This fixes the direction of mathematical dependence.
