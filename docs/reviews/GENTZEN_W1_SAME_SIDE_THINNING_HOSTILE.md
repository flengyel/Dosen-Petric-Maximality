   Atomized predicates equal AtomicFormula E ∧ f.Atomized. Failure mode
   unused_hypothesis / unsupported_generalization; evidence derivation.
   Four explicit iff lemmas prove this exact boundary; compound E remains
   permitted by denotation/rank but yields a nonatomized example. The derived
   constructor theorem from hf includes hE. No unconditional atomization
   claim from hf alone is made.
3. Anchor: report “Exact local rank equations” and continuation. Claim:
   targeted endpoint loss n−1 and inserted loss 1. Failure mode
   unsupported_generalization; evidence structural_argument. These two local
   comparisons do not prove all-site rank decrease, quotient invariance,
   Reducibility or termination in arbitrary proof contexts. Those tasks and
   the source's broader coherence appeal remain open explicitly.

No counterexample to the four scoped claims is asserted. The packaged
