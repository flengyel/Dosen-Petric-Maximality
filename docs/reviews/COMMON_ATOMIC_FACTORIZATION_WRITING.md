## Definition-order and notation audit

The existing normal forms, Term/Equation/Derives/Hom and contextual identities
precede the new work. Positive is defined inductively before any middle term
is claimed positive. Traversal lists and atomicCount precede extraction and
termination. AtomicSplit gives typed context endpoints and a positive preterm;
its exact list fields distinguish occurrences even when labels repeat.

Port block, expand and the two factor maps are defined before their naturality
and separation results. Selection data precede keep/absent padding. The source
initial f_i' and completed f_i'' are distinguished before the common proof.
Masks and tagged embeddings precede complete support. PositiveCoherence is
stated with raw-term quantifiers and ambient Reduced.Hom equality before its
conditional use. These are confirmed definitions, not missing-definition findings.

The same x/y denote original occurrence tags and u/v denote expanded tags.
Embeddings are functions on positions, not maps on equal labels. Empty lists
have identity factors; Empty occurrence sets give no support witnesses.
“First” in extraction means first in raw syntax traversal; composition executes
the left semicolon argument first. No statement identifies the raw factorization
algorithm with a function on quotient arrows.

## Prioritized wording

Retain: “For every two parallel raw terms, one construction supplies the same
expanded endpoints, prefix and suffix and two checked positive middle terms.”
Follow the factor equality by: “The explicit zero masks remove all auxiliary
input and output edges.” State the stronger support equivalence, rather than
using “same pairs” without coordinates. For transfer retain: “Positive-fragment
coherence, if proved, implies Reduced.Faithful P.” These are the actual wording
and quantifier repairs applied in the report/maps. No manuscript revision is
needed for this checkpoint.
